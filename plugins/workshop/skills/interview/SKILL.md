Interview the user until you share an understanding of what they want. Walk each branch of the decision tree, settling first the decisions others depend on.

## Asking

Ask one question at a time, give your recommended answer, and wait for the reply before forming the next. The decisions are the user's; never settle one for them.

A question the user cannot answer leaves with a named step that would settle it: an experiment to run, a thing to build first, a person to ask.

## Closing

When nothing is left to ask, write the understanding out in enough detail that the user can find where it is wrong. A restatement they can only agree with checks nothing. A correction reopens the questions; only the user's confirmation ends the interview. Write nothing to files.
